Privacy Policy

Last updated August 13, 2026

What this app is

kawakun-tiktok-autopost is a personal-use tool built by an individual developer. It publishes short video content — comedy skits featuring an animated otter character, "Kawakun" — to a single TikTok account that the developer owns and operates (@kawakun_exp). It is not distributed to other users and does not serve any account other than its own.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to TikTok users, viewers, or anyone other than the app's own operator.

The only data the app handles is:

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok for the single authorized account, stored locally and used solely to call the Content Posting API.
  • Video files the developer already owns, queued locally before upload.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, trackers, or analytics of any kind.
  • No sharing or selling of data to third parties.
  • No access to any TikTok account other than the one the operator explicitly authorizes.

Data retention

Access tokens are kept only as long as needed to keep the app authorized and are deleted if authorization is revoked. Deleting the app's stored credentials removes all data the app holds.

Questions about this policy can be sent to kawakun.exp.everyday@gmail.com.
